Why seaweed?

Most Indian skin care routines are built on plants from land, like aloe, haldi, rose and neem, or on one lab-made active, like niacinamide or vitamin C. Repêchage is built on seaweed, hand-harvested off the coasts of Brittany in France and Maine in the USA.

42trace elements
18amino acids

Found in one seaweed, Laminaria digitata, the cornerstone of Repêchage since 1980, as Repêchage describes it.
