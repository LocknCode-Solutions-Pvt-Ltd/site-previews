Repêchage in India: The JB Skincare Story

By JB SkincareThe partnership since 2012, year by year

In short: yes, Repêchage is available in India. JB Skincare has been the exclusive authorized importer and distributor of Repêchage for India since 2012, and imports the range under an import license issued by the CDSCO.

You can buy Repêchage home care on jbskincare.in, and book Repêchage professional facials at salons, spas and clinics that JB Skincare supplies and trains. This is the story of how the brand came to India, the people behind JB Skincare, and how to make sure the Repêchage you buy is genuine.

How it started: the founders of JB Skincare

JB Skincare was founded by a husband-and-wife team, Jyoti Sapra and Bikram Sapra. When Repêchage announced the partnership in April 2012, it described Jyoti as a highly experienced and trained cosmetologist who owned two high-end salons and spas in New Jersey, in the United States. An Indian native, she had used Repêchage in her own treatment rooms for more than ten years, and she saw a need for this caliber of professional skin care in India.

"Repêchage has always consistently given me amazing results, and my clients love the seaweed-based facials," Jyoti said at the time. "We are very excited to bring the highly reputed Repêchage skin care products to India for the first time!"

Bikram Sapra took charge of the new distribution business and went on to lead it as Managing Director. "We are thrilled to be appointed as their exclusive distributor for India," he said in 2012. "Repêchage products are very beneficial in protecting the skin from the harsh climatic conditions in India." At the start, the couple divided their time between New Jersey and India.

From the first day, the plan was bigger than selling products. Repêchage and JB Skincare set out to bring serious professional skin care to India and to give spas, salons and clinics the education and business support to use it well. That idea of education first still shapes how JB Skincare works today.

Today

JB Skincare is the exclusive authorized importer and distributor of both Repêchage and VOESH for India. It supplies dermatologists, skin clinics, salons and spas across the country, trains their teams, and sells Repêchage and VOESH home care directly on jbskincare.in.

Who is Repêchage?

Repêchage is a professional skin care brand founded in 1980 by Lydia Sarfati in the United States. Repêchage describes itself as a pioneer of seaweed treatments and cosmetics in the United States, and its best-known treatment, the Four Layer Facial, is used in spas and salons around the world. The products are made in the United States, and Repêchage works through skin care professionals in more than 50 countries.

The seaweed is the heart of the range. If you want to understand why it matters for skin, read our guide to what seaweed does for your skin.

How to check that a Repêchage product is genuine

Most problems with imported skin care come from unofficial channels: products that were stored badly, are close to their expiry date, or are not what the label says. These checks help:

  1. Know where it came from. Buy on jbskincare.in or from a professional who buys from JB Skincare. If a salon sells you a product, it is fair to ask where they source it.
  2. Look for the importer details. In India, imported packaged products have to show the name and address of the importer on the pack. For Repêchage, the importer is JB Skincare. A product with no Indian importer details is worth questioning.
  3. Check the MRP, batch and dates. The pack should carry a printed MRP, a batch or lot number and the manufacturing or expiry details. Unopened Repêchage products have a shelf life of three years unless the pack says otherwise.
  4. Check the seal and the print. The product should be sealed, with clean, consistent printing. Look closely at the name: genuine packs spell it Repêchage, and each product name matches the range, such as C-Serum Seaweed Filtrate or Algo Mist Hydrating Seaweed and Mineral Water Spray.
  5. Be careful with prices that look too good. A very large discount on a premium imported product can mean old stock, poor storage or a copy.
  6. Notice anything unusual once opened. A change in smell, color or texture compared with what you know, or separation in a cream, is a reason to stop using it and ask.

If you are not sure about a product, send clear photos of the pack, the batch number and where you bought it to info@jbskincare.in, or call 011-47504498.

Why buying from the official importer matters

  • Storage and freshness. Skin care is sensitive to heat and time. Stock that comes through the official channel is imported directly from the manufacturer.
  • The right product for you. The JB Skincare team is trained in the Repêchage range and can help you choose, which matters for a range with several collections for different skin types.
  • Support if something goes wrong. If an order arrives damaged or incorrect, JB Skincare asks for an unboxing video and will replace the product or give store credit.
