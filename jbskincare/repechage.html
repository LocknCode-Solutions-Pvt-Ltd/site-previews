Repêchage: beauty from the sea, since 1980

Repêchage was founded in 1980 by Lydia Sarfati, a pioneer of seaweed skin care, and today works through skin care professionals in more than 50 countries. JB Skincare has been its exclusive importer and distributor in India since 2012.

Who is Repêchage?

Seaweed is the heart of the range

Repêchage describes itself as a pioneer of seaweed treatments and cosmetics in the United States. Its best-known treatment, the Four Layer Facial, is used in spas and salons around the world, and its products are made in the United States.

  • Founded in 1980 by Lydia Sarfati
  • Used by skin care professionals in more than 50 countries
  • Imported into India by JB Skincare, under a CDSCO import license

The partnership

Repêchage appoints J B Skin Care its exclusive distributor for India. Co-founder Jyoti Sapra had used Repêchage in her own New Jersey salons for more than ten years.

Professional Beauty, New Delhi

JB shows Repêchage to spa and salon professionals, with the Biolight Miracle Facial demonstrated live.

2016

Distributor of the Year

100 professionals in Mumbai

JB and Lydia Sarfati host more than 100 beauty professionals at the St. Regis Mumbai to launch new professional treatments.

Trained salons across India

The signature treatment

The Four Layer Facial

The facial that started it all in 1980: a seaweed serum, a hydrating cream with a long massage, a fresh seaweed mask and a mineral mask that sets over it. Salons, spas and clinics supplied by JB offer it alongside the Biolight Miracle Facial and the Vita Cura 5 Phase Firming Facial.
