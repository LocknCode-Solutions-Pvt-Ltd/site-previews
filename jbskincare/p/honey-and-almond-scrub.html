What it does

This scrub gently removes rough patches and impurities, revealing smoother, more radiant skin. It hydrates while exfoliating, helping to lock in moisture for a supple and rejuvenated complexion. With regular use, it restores a youthful glow, minimizes the appearance of imperfections, and transforms your skincare routine into a luxurious, pampering experience.

How to use

On cleansed skin, apply the scrub using moistened fingertips and gently massage with firm, circular motions. Focus on areas with enlarged pores and blackheads for optimal results. Leave it on for 10-15 minutes as a deep cleansing mask, or rinse off in the shower for quick smoothing and cleansing.

What is inside

This scrub features St. John’s Wort to soothe and tone, honey to hydrate and calm, and almond meal for gentle yet effective exfoliation. Oatmeal adds a soothing touch while helping absorb excess oils, leaving your skin soft, smooth, and refreshed. A perfect blend for a radiant complexion.
