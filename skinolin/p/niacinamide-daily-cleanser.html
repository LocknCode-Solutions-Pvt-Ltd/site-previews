Questions

Is the Niacinamide Daily Cleanser suitable for oily skin?

Yes. It cleans away excess oil without over-drying and is suitable for all skin types.

Is it a good face wash for dry skin?

Yes. It is soap-free and cleans without stripping the skin's natural moisture, so it works as a gentle face wash for dry skin. Follow with a hydrating serum and moisturizer.

Can this cleanser brighten the skin?

Niacinamide (vitamin B3) helps improve uneven skin tone and enhances your skin's natural glow over time. For best results, use it daily and wear sunscreen every morning.

Is it gentle enough to use twice a day?

Yes. It is designed for morning and night use. Use lukewarm water, massage for 1 to 2 minutes and rinse thoroughly.

Which ingredients are in the Niacinamide Daily Cleanser?

It contains niacinamide, aloe vera, chamomile, gotu kola, red clover, rose water and ratanjot oil to cleanse, soothe and nourish the skin.

Does this cleanser help reduce pores?

Niacinamide helps minimize the appearance of pores and smooth skin texture with regular use.

How do I use the Niacinamide Daily Cleanser?

Apply a generous amount to your damp face and neck, massage gently in circular motions for 1 to 2 minutes, rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water, then follow with your serum, moisturizer and sunscreen.
