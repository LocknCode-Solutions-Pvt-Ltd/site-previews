Why waterless

Most sunscreens start with water. Ours doesn't.

Look at the back of most face sunscreens and water (aqua) is the first ingredient. Skinolin's SPF 50 has none: it is anhydrous, so the bottle holds only the formula itself.

  • SPF 50 PA++++, broad spectrum UVA and UVB
  • Water resistant for 60 minutes, as on the pack
